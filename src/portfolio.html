About

The
person
behind
the work.

I build things that run and feel right.

For nearly a decade I worked as a software engineer — learning how systems are designed, how teams are led, and how products get from idea to execution. But the whole time, I was building something else on the side.

A virtual rap competition that became Indonesia's first of its kind — drawing 2,500+ CCV live viewers and running for three annual editions. A digital entertainment brand I built from zero to 104K+ YouTube subscribers and 11K+ monthly Spotify listeners. Music campaigns commissioned by ASUS and Hololive Indonesia. Fan activation events at Indonesia's largest conventions. All of it produced, directed, and managed by me.

Eventually it became undeniable: the creative and strategic work was where I actually thrived. So I made the move — fully and intentionally.

Today I work as a Creative Project Manager and Entertainment Strategist — bringing my engineering background with me, not leaving it behind. I understand how things are built. I understand how audiences think. And I know how to hold a creative vision together from brief to execution without letting either the art or the logistics slip.

If you're building something that needs to be both beautifully creative and strategically sharp — Let's Talk
